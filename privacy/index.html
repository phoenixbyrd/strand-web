Privacy & security

The only DNA app that can't leak your DNA.

Most genetics services upload your genome to their servers, where it can be breached, subpoenaed, or sold off in a bankruptcy. Strandis architected so that's impossible: your file is read and analyzed on your device, and never sent anywhere.

✓

Parsed in your browser

Reading and interpreting your file happens in client-side JavaScript on the upload page. The bytes of your genome never travel to a server.

✓

Stored only on your device

So a refresh doesn't lose your report, your file is cached in your browser's local IndexedDB — on your machine, under your control.

✓

Deletable in one click

The 'Delete data' button in your report wipes the local copy immediately. Close the tab and there's nothing left behind on our side.

✓

Nothing sold, ever

There is no genome database to monetize. We don't share, sell, or use your data for research. There's no fine print because there's no data to give away.

Health disclaimer

Strand provides educational, research-grade interpretations of consumer genotyping files (for example 23andMe, AncestryDNA, MyHeritage, or FamilyTreeDNA exports). It is not a medical device, is not a diagnostic test, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consumer arrays read only a fraction of the genome and can contain errors, no-calls, and strand-orientation mismatches. A "typical" result does not rule a condition out. A flagged result is not a diagnosis and is not a reason to start, stop, or change a medication. Do not make health, reproductive, insurance, or medication decisions from this report. Confirm anything that matters with a licensed clinician or a certified genetic counselor, using a clinical-grade test when appropriate. If you have a medical emergency, seek emergency care. By using Strand you acknowledge that you understand these limits.
